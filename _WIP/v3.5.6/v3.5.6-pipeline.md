## The pipeline, step by step

### At shape load — once, size-free (steps 1–4)

1. **Trace** the outline (cutouts from the alpha edge; presets/generators direct).
2. **Centre** — measured once, a fixed property of the shape, scaled linearly ever after.
   **The centre is DERIVED from the legal area, not chosen by a mode button** (ruled 16:5x):
   - measure the 12mm legal area and its masses (the bake already does);
   - **one mass** → the centre is that mass's own centre (the true middle of the usable material);
   - **several masses** → the governed TOP mass anchors — Top-small law (smallest mass in the
     upper half; gravity first; a bottom sliver can never rule);
   - a mass centre is by definition inside seatable material → the void-anchor failure
     (Box/Weight/Core averaging into a neck) is structurally impossible.
   The six mode buttons remain as inspection views only. **Every mode converges to the same
   final result** ("I don't even have to switch centering — it must be the same because the
   fine-tune must make it so").
3. **Segments** — the legal area's islands and box, measured once at reference size and
   RETAINED (the box's proportions are the classifier's input).
4. **Classification** — family (SQUARE fills its box · ROUND = rounded box · TRIANGLE =
   partial box: triangle, diamond, T, L, waisted) and the per-band frame (band id = dominant
   axis line count; minor axis in proportion). Shown in the admin Classifier panel — name of
   class and kind — for validation by eye. Canvas dot-overlays are rejected ("orange slops").

### At band click (steps 5–12)

5. **Library lookup** — the band's layouts come from a LITERAL LAYOUT LIBRARY: a readable,
   precomputed table per family × frame, each entry a named layout with exact nodes
   (frame · ring · corners · tee (mirrored on even widths, apex on real nodes only) · ell ·
   diagonal). No generation at solve time; editable like canon. **The single and the pair are
   tried in every band regardless of class.** The Perimeter belt is expressed in the table
   (a 3×3 under the belt IS the 8-ring), never applied as a runtime afterthought.
6. **Place** each layout dead on the centre.
7. **Wrap seals** — the exact Clipper contact solve: deflate by 12mm → intersect translated
   regions → bisect to 0.01mm. If the centred position becomes unlawful the layout shifts
   ONLY the exact mm the wrap demands — the cove-dip. Wrap is the law: never grow the shape
   to stay pinned on the centre; a variant with slack is not a wrap.
8. **Band membership** — a layout whose contact size falls outside the band belongs to another
   band and is not offered here.
9. **Anti-flap measure** — subtract every magnet's hold disc (pitch/√2 circumradius) from the
   shape; find unheld patches touching the outline; any bare edge RUN over 96mm violates the
   perimeter law ("every minimum 96mm of perimeter must be pinned").
10. **Guard — fallback only** ("the class itself must be it — but if not enough, use the
    clipper2 fix that subtracts and ellipses"): force a magnet onto the lattice node at the
    WORST unpinned outline point (never the patch centroid), top first — gravity — and re-wrap.
    Repeat until clean or impossible. The result is marked REPAIRED and never outranks a
    layout that held natively.
11. **Fine-tune** — the small adjustment ("it could have computed the small adjustment to make
    it wrap and be closer to center"): constrained contact solves under a shrinking shift cap;
    spend size only while each mm buys MORE than a mm of centring; spend capped at a quarter
    pitch. Every candidate is a true contact solve — pressed, no slack. A tune that re-exposes
    an edge is rejected: the anti-flap law outranks centring.
12. Repeat 6–11 for every library entry.

### The judge — one ordered pass (steps 13–16)

13. **96mm law** — offers still violating die while any clean offer exists. Exception: the
    single and the pair are judged by centring/balance, not perimeter — and the exception is
    SCOPED to bands whose frame carries ≤ 2 lines (B1/B2). A pair never lands B3+ where the
    class demands more.
14. **Coverage dominance** — within 1mm of size, more magnets is strictly more coverage; the
    lesser offer is dropped, not outranked. Judged at contact AND re-judged on final sizes
    (guard and tune move counts and sizes).
15. **Best class match** — native library layouts outrank repaired ones outrank
    reveal-discovered ones ("why do we have these two options if the 148mm provides the best
    matching to the class").
16. **Landing** — within the winning tier: fewest magnets, then least shift. Identical finals
    collapse to one chip. All surviving offers stay visible as the ladder.

### Fallback (only when the library yields nothing that holds)

17. The reveal scan — centre-rules seating at 1mm steps across the band, distinct layouts
    wrapped and judged by the same judge. Discovery serves only where the catalogue fails.

## Standing rulings folded in (with their moments)

- **Reversal** (11:08): size/band in, count OUT. The magnet count is never an input.
- **Wrap solved, not scanned** (08-24 17:07): Clipper exact; "nothing fully fits" cannot occur.
- **Measure the centre once** (13:56): the centre is a shape property; only qualification is
  size-dependent. The 2mm-mesh jitter and governor flips were re-measurement noise.
- **Group centre = shape centre unless the wrap demands otherwise** (14:02): deviation is the
  exception, computed as `centreOffMM`, part of the fine-tune, never free-floating.
- **96mm perimeter law** (10:03) · **gravity first, top flap always** (10:08) ·
  **1–2 magnets are the exception — centring/diagonal pairs decide** (10:16, 15:56).
- **Layouts are presets, not discoveries** (14:40): "shapes are undefined cause they are
  random — segment recognises and classes the shape; the layouts for each class are pre-sets
  precomputed variations"; bands × presets = "100% certainty… can precompute all".
- **Magnets exist only at lattice nodes** — no half-pitch apexes; even-width tees are the
  mirrored pair.
- **Coverage dominance** (14:22): no offer with unprotected areas when a same-size offer
  covers them. There is NO enforced magnet count anywhere — count is always an output.
- **Convergence** (15:47): any centring start must fine-tune to the same required result —
  wrap / closest to centre / no flap.
- **Flap is deleted** (12:32): the magnet disc IS the allowance. **Padding locked at 12mm.**
- **T-shape thickness rule** (14:50, recorded, not yet in the library): thinner than 48mm →
  1 row/column; 48–96 → 2; a segment taller AND wider than each 48mm step adds a magnet there.

   legal area (one mass → its centre; several → governed top mass); connect the classifier;
   Classifier panel returns.

   by Dan in the panel.
3. **Pipeline swap** — library lookup replaces the reveal as the layout source; reveal demoted
   to fallback.
4. **The judge** — steps 9–16 as ONE ordered pass (guard, tune, 96mm law with scoped
   exception, dominance, class-match tiers, landing).

results written before the code and screenshots on every landing. No "done" before the matrix
passes.
