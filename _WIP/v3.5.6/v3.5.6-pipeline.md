# v3.5.6 Pipeline

## Step 1 — Segmentation, legal area, class

Once per shape, at load, size-free. Scale never changes it.

1. Erode the traced outline by 12mm. What remains is the legal area — where a magnet centre
   may sit.
2. Measure the legal area: its islands, their masses, and the segment box.
3. Read the class from the segments: family (square · round · triangle) and kind, plus the
   frame per band.
4. Show the result in the admin panel — name of class and kind — for validation by eye.
