## STEP 3 · The engine answers by knowing instead of by stumbling — THE MVP

**Why.** The contract. Today the engine is a size sweep with a hidden max-count winner: on a 168mm
square it builds four lawful registrations — 16, 12, 12, 9 magnets — and keeps only the 16 before
wrap sees any of them, which Dan's brief forbids by name.

**Do — one change, no dual path.**
- **One door.** `grid-magnet.ts` runs segment → classify → match → wrap → an unsorted attempt ledger.
  No sort, no default landing, no witness, no judge.
- **Headless.** That door is callable from a plain test with no browser. The worker becomes transport
  and cache only; the page reaches the engine through an adapter. This is what makes "test it" mean
  something other than clicking the bench, and it is the lane's stated deliverable.
- **Classifier.** Ordered frame from the union of all live masses — never the outer box, never the
  governing mass. Ceiling from the live band table; the `1|2|3|4|5` type, the countdown and the clamp
  go. Returns **every** candidate on the frame with its evidence, marked ambiguous: 8 of 24 frame
  groups are cross-class, so a single winner would be an invented filter.
- **Matcher.** Candidates by band + ordered frame, returning `{ entry, view }` — one canonical record,
  the matcher says which way round. This is what lets a landscape shape receive a portrait layout.
  `ROTATION_VIEWS` / `D4_VIEWS` proved through `transformLayout` before use.
- **Placement.** The full rigid population enters `wrapGroup`, which already computes the exact set of
  lawful origins. A null wrap is evidence — never permission to drop a node.
- **Centring stays** as the governed anchor: `heldAt`/`pickOrigin` only break ties among origins that
  are already lawful, so it filters nothing.
- **Deleted in the same change.** `grid-magnet-wrap-compute.ts` (sweep, ladder, dedupe, judge
  composition) · `units/judge.ts` · `defaultLanding` · `registerLayout` and its four-parity winner ·
  `applyCoverage` · `fallbackRevealSizes` · `bestSeatedCandidate` · `grid-magnet-class.ts` and
  `shapeFamilyOf` · `grid-magnet-compute.ts` · `grid-magnet-logic.ts` · both stride decimators · the
  dead two-magnet floor · the silent invalid-band fallback · both contact thresholds · and the
  padding resolved at eleven sites, replaced by one resolver at the door returning the released 12
  with no unit defaulting or clamping.
- **Shell.** One card per attempt: class, frame, requested/placed/omitted, wrap size, the band it
  actually landed in, outcome. Auto-select only when exactly one attempt wrapped in the asked band.

**DONE WHEN.**
1. A plain test, no browser, calls the door for a shape and receives the attempt ledger — pasted.
2. `grep` proves zero production references to every deleted symbol, by name.
3. On the bench at the exact commit: a shape whose right answer Dan knows shows its attempts,
   unordered — screenshot, serving tree and commit named.
4. The 168mm square shows **four** registrations where it showed one.
5. Suite, typecheck, scoped lint clean.

**Not in it.** Scoring, ordering, coverage, orientation preference, next-best, the belt as a visible
step, the 96mm garment lattice.

# MODULE COMPLETION PLANS

Where each part stands, and what finished means for it.

**Catalogue** — *contract landed, no consumer.* v3, pitch-aware, non-null band, one canon population
per frame, 254/92/47 records. **Missing:** a consumer. **Finished when** the matcher reads it and the
engine wraps what it returns (step 3).

**Classifier** — *a label, not a decision.* Measures the outer outline's box, counts to five and
clamps, and its only consumer is a display readout — delete it today and every answer is identical.
**Missing:** the mass-union measurement, the derived ceiling, an honest multi-candidate result, a
consumer. **Finished when** shape in → ordered frame, band and every candidate out, and the match
uses it (step 3).

**Matcher** — *exists, dormant, wrong key.* `catalogueCandidates` has no production caller and filters
on the invented `fill < 0.68` family. **Missing:** band + ordered frame as the key, `{ entry, view }`
as the answer. **Finished when** a landscape shape receives a portrait layout (step 3).

**Layout** — *correct home, hidden selection.* Owns the seat rule, the lattice, the four
registrations, the belt, the fallback sizes; silently keeps one registration in four by count.
**Finished when** only the seat rule survives (steps 2 and 3).

**Segment** — *done.* Legal area, islands, masses, holes, all hole-aware. **Missing:** nothing
functional. Both decimators go in step 3; the mesh resolution gets disclosed in the contract because
the classifier will depend on it near cell boundaries.

**Wrap** — *complete. Do not touch.* Exact contact solve, holes honoured, origin solved exactly.
Boundary only: `paddingMM` required, no floor fallback, raw unrounded size out, the adapter rounds.
Dan: *"the wrap law cannot be relieved in any of the cases."*

**Centring** — *ruled, and it stays.* Six modes, four governors, baked once and scaled. In the MVP it
supplies wrap's anchor and nothing else. Mass depth kept on Dan's word; the natural replacement —
"material that can hold more than one magnet" — is derivable from the pitch and needs no dial, but
that is a product change and waits.

**Judge** — *33 lines of a much larger job, and it leaves in step 3.* **Finished when**, post-MVP, it
carries the coverage measure (longest unpinned arc — built and reverted as collateral; on an L it
separated B4 offers 386mm versus 101mm; it must be cyclic and must measure hole rings), orientation
match, and Dan's order: exact optimal first, everything else next-best and scored.

**Next-best** — *not built.* Dan's method, not the sweep: find the minimum count that holds → propose;
the maximum count → propose; the class canon's count → propose. Three targeted solves against 48
blind ones. **Finished when** those three probes replace the sweep, which is recoverable from
`62843c61`.

**Pipeline and adapters** — *do not exist; the worker is both.* **Finished when** one serialisable
call runs headless, the worker is transport and cache, and the page reaches the engine through an
adapter (step 3).

**Spec** — *values, with defects.* Needs the FILTERS block holding every number that narrows the
search, live-consumer values only. **Defects:** `FIELD_POSITIONS_PER_AXIS` still reads one axis for
both, so the engine caps every shape at 420mm on a board that is 384×480.

**Shell** — *a bench, and it stays one.* **Finished when** it renders the attempt ledger and nothing
else: no ordering, no default, no controls the engine does not read.
