## 3. Centre

1. Compute the governed anchor from the admin-selected Centre mode.
2. Align the grid's magnetic cell structure to that anchor. Filled cells and empty alignment cells both count as grid alignment positions.
3. The magnetic disc/cell atom is 24 mm; the lattice is 48 mm centre-to-centre. Both are active Spec/admin values, not a hidden scheduling law.
4. Alignment follows group parity:
   - one magnet: magnet centre to governed anchor;
   - a pair: governed anchor at the midpoint/gap between its two filled cells, horizontal or vertical as available;
   - a 2×2 group: governed anchor at the group's middle cell/gap;
   - larger groups follow the same node/gap principle.
5. Centre measurement is based on the selected group's extents/midpoint, not the arithmetic mean of an unrelated full seated field.

Mode behaviour:

- **Flat/equal mode:** Centre is immovable. Fit and Magnet Addition must resolve around it.
- **Scoring mode:** Centre occupies the admin-selected position in the priority chain. The existing scoring search may select another measured registration; this is the only current adaptive shift.
- A separate post-centre adaptive-fit/shift subsystem is explicitly deferred.

## 5. Fit / Wrap

Every candidate first satisfies the universal safe-area rule. Fit then uses the admin-selected mode:

- `group`
- `single`
- `double`
- `most`
- `all`

These are exposed inputs, not hidden policy.

Rules fixed by Dan:

1. `group` uses the invisible padded bounding box of the selected magnet group. That straight square/rectangle is the selected contact body: it must stay inside the complete supplied material and may press cleanly against the shape's inner edges within the active configured allowance.
2. `double` requires two touched magnets on opposite sides of the group. The pair may be horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
3. Disc-contact modes allow discs to sink into cavities while every disc still remains safely inside the complete material.
4. Tangency/contact evidence belongs to the selected Fit mode; it is not silently imposed as an all-disc universal gate.
5. Flap/contact allowance is the configured admin input used by the selected Fit judgement.

For Group Fit, flap is the remaining unsupported outer space around the centred padded group, not a universal per-disc tangency gate. Disc tangency may be contact evidence under a selected disc-contact mode, but every disc remains subject to universal safe-area containment.

Adaptive Fit beyond the existing scoring search is later experimental work and is not part of this build.

## 9. Module ownership

- **Spec:** all configuration, options, bounds and defaults.
- **Compute:** geometry and neutral measurements needed by the current candidate and procedure. Compute may enforce universal physical eligibility such as safe-area containment; it does not rank or choose a winner.
- **Logic:** applies the active Centre/Fit/Magnet Addition or scoring rules to measurements. It contains no geometry kernel or duplicated Spec.
- **Engine:** streams the configured search and orchestrates Compute → Logic. It does not prebuild an unbounded neutral population.
- **Worker/bridge:** transport, caching and request/result wiring only.
- **UI:** renders Spec controls and all returned results; no solver logic.

Keep the existing scaffold and move/reuse proven bodies rather than creating another solver.
