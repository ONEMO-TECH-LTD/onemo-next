## T1 — Flap is a wrap tolerance only. Remove it from every other role.

**The rule.** Flap is the invisible allowance a disc wears: it sets where "the material
presses on this magnet" is measured from, and nothing else. It is a *measurement offset*,
never a legality test and never a pass/fail gate.

**Why.** An allowance about supported material must not decide where a magnet may sit, and
must not delete lawful layouts. Today it does both, which is the direct cause of a band and
Free giving different answers for the same shape at the same size, and of the
`"nothing fully fits at this flap — best seated shown"` banner appearing while the layout is
findable by hand. Brief §5.4 (contact evidence belongs to the *selected* Fit mode, never a
silent all-disc gate) and §10 (do not preserve band-only Wrap, or Centre/Wrap differences
between modes).

### What flap does today — three jobs, one legitimate

| # | Job | Where | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wrap tolerance: `reach = padding + flap`, the line the disc gap is measured from | `grid-magnet.ts:127` | **KEEP** — this is what flap is |
| 2 | Seating legality: `seatMarginMM = flap` shrinks the legal magnet-centre area, so raising flap deletes magnets | `grid-magnet.ts:308`, `:350`, consumed at `:136-137`; worker `solve.worker.ts:76`, `:120` | **REMOVE** |
| 3 | Band pass/fail gate: all-disc `maxPress(reach) <= stepMM` — one floating disc kills the whole size | `grid-magnet.ts:319`, `:330-332` | **REMOVE** |

### The change

- **Seating is padding only.** The seat predicate takes `spotRadiusOf(pad)` with no margin
  term. Delete the `seatMarginMM` config field and all five call sites; a magnet's legality
  is the 12mm safe area (brief §4), identical in Free, band and manual.
- **Delete the all-disc rigid gate** in `bandWalk`. Contact evidence returns later as the
  *selected* Fit mode (brief §5) — `group | single | double | most | all` — not as a
  hardcoded universal test. `maxPressMM` becomes unused by the band walk; keep the function,
  it is the per-disc contact measurement the Fit modes will need.
- **Flap keeps exactly one consumer**: `reach` in the wrap measurement, plus the dashed ring
  the canvas draws. Same value, same meaning, in every mode.

### Consequences to expect (verify, do not assume)

- Raising flap no longer removes magnets anywhere.
- A band stops reporting "nothing fits" — it reports the layouts that seat, as Free does.
- Free and band seat identically at the same size for the same config, at any flap.
- Band ladders get longer: sizes previously rejected by the gate now qualify. Whether they
  are *lawful* becomes the Fit mode's decision, not flap's.
