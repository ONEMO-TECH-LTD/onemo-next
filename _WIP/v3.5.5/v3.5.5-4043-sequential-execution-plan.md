# v3.5.5 — 4043 Sequential Execution Plan

## Authority

- Baseline: clean `affb2d7e3a647130083f83d0384a282aa60ffe97`, byte-identical to the selected 4043 engine.
- Finish the existing engine; do not create another engine, geometry platform, module hierarchy or parallel API.
- The three laws are **Centre**, **Fit / Wrap** and **Magnet Addition**. Scaling is only the configured search action used to find the shape size where those laws become possible; Scale is not scored or treated as a fourth law.
- Before any law is judged, Compute derives the safe magnet-centre area by inward-eroding the supplied material outline by the configured spot radius (default 12mm). Implement that erosion with the existing equivalent predicate — centre inside material and nearest outline distance at least the radius — not a constructed offset polygon or geometry platform. It is recomputed at every searched scale and never changes the manufactured or displayed outline.
- Centre first finds the governed shape centre. Grid alignment uses the configured spot diameter as its atomic cell (default 24mm): filled magnet positions and empty alignment cells share the same structure. Odd runs may place a filled cell on the centre; even runs place the empty midpoint between the central magnets on it. This covers one magnet, a pair, 2x2, six magnets and larger groups without a special-case catalogue.
- Fit always requires every disc centre inside the safe area. Its Spec-owned, admin-editable policy is `group | single | double | most | all`, default `double`. `group` uses the invisible bounding box of the selected magnet centres expanded by the configured spot radius as the Wrap surface: the box must remain inside the shape and an opposing pair of its edges must press within the configured allowance. `single` requires one touched disc; `double` an opposing touched pair; `most` a strict majority including an opposing pair; `all` every disc. Opposing magnets lie across the governed group centre and may be horizontal, vertical or diagonal; two contacts on the same side do not qualify.
- Flat/equal mode keeps the governed Centre alignment immovable and applies Centre, Fit and Magnet Addition without a priority order. Scoring mode uses the six selectable priority orders over those same three measurements; it may choose another already-enumerated alignment when Centre is later in the chain. There is no second adaptive-shift or recentering pass.
- Magnet Addition creates the product tiers. Every next tier adds at least one magnet; a balanced pair is preferred when lawful, but a single added magnet remains valid for an irregular shape. Magnet count is never an early candidate filter.
- Keep module ownership: Spec values/types; Compute measurements; Logic enforcement/ranking; Engine orchestration; Bridge/worker transport/cache; UI controls/presentation.
- Every result-affecting calibration value lives in Spec and is wired to the admin UI. Positioning remains separate from scoring.
