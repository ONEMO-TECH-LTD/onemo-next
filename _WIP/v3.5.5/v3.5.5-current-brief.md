1. apply Centre, Fit and Magnet Addition as one global procedure;

## 2. The three laws and the search action

The laws are:

1. **Centre**
2. **Fit / Wrap**
3. **Magnet Addition**

**Scale is not a law.** Scaling searches up or down, within the configured size bands and configured steps, for sizes at which Centre, Fit and the next magnet population can pass.

The engine applies the three laws while each arrangement is constructed and judged. It must not first materialise a raw population of every imaginable grid subset and defer the laws to later tasks.

Source: `M24:51-58`, `M24:123-140`, `M24:907-959`.

## 3. Centre

1. Compute the governed anchor from the admin-selected Centre mode.
2. Align the grid's magnetic cell structure to that anchor. Filled cells and empty alignment cells both count as grid alignment positions.
3. The magnetic disc/cell atom is 24 mm; the lattice is 48 mm centre-to-centre. Both are active Spec/admin values, not a hidden scheduling law.
4. Alignment follows group parity:
   - one magnet: magnet centre to governed anchor;
   - a pair: governed anchor at the midpoint/gap between its two filled cells, horizontal or vertical as available;
   - a 2×2 group: governed anchor at the group's middle cell/gap;
   - larger groups follow the same node/gap principle.
5. Centre measurement is based on the selected group's extents/midpoint, not the arithmetic mean of an unrelated full seated field.

Mode behaviour:

- **Flat/equal mode:** Centre is immovable. Fit and Magnet Addition must resolve around it.
- **Scoring mode:** Centre occupies the admin-selected position in the priority chain. The existing scoring search may select another measured registration; this is the only current adaptive shift.
- A separate post-centre adaptive-fit/shift subsystem is explicitly deferred.

Source: `Q23:2298-2338`; `M24:51-58`, `M24:88-114`.

## 4. Universal safe application area

Every magnet disc must remain inside the supplied material.

Compute the safe magnet-centre area dynamically by eroding every supplied material boundary by the configured spot radius/padding. The current physical example is 12 mm, but the runtime value comes from Spec/admin. Supplied holes remain material boundaries; they are not dropped by scaling or judgement.

This is universal eligibility, not a scoring preference and not an optional Fit mode.

Source: `M24:139-140`; the saved permanent result requires every disc to stay inside the supplied shape (`Q23:3192-3205`).

## 5. Fit / Wrap

Every candidate first satisfies the universal safe-area rule. Fit then uses the admin-selected mode:

- `group`
- `single`
- `double`
- `most`
- `all`

These are exposed inputs, not hidden policy.

Rules fixed by Dan:

1. `group` uses the invisible padded bounding box of the selected magnet group. That straight square/rectangle is the selected contact body: it must stay inside the complete supplied material and may press cleanly against the shape's inner edges within the active configured allowance.
2. `double` requires two touched magnets on opposite sides of the group. The pair may be horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
3. Disc-contact modes allow discs to sink into cavities while every disc still remains safely inside the complete material.
4. Tangency/contact evidence belongs to the selected Fit mode; it is not silently imposed as an all-disc universal gate.
5. Flap/contact allowance is the configured admin input used by the selected Fit judgement.

For Group Fit, flap is the remaining unsupported outer space around the centred padded group, not a universal per-disc tangency gate. Disc tangency may be contact evidence under a selected disc-contact mode, but every disc remains subject to universal safe-area containment.

Adaptive Fit beyond the existing scoring search is later experimental work and is not part of this build.

Source: `Q23:2298-2338`; `M24:4-41`, `M24:88-114`.

## 6. Magnet Addition and size-band search

Within each configured band:

1. search only the sizes, grid registrations, pitch, coverage, magnet plan, Fit mode, allowance and other values permitted by the active Spec/admin inputs;
2. centre and judge each arrangement through the full three-law procedure as it is encountered;
3. reject a failing arrangement immediately; retain a lawful one immediately;
4. begin each band at the smallest magnet population that can pass Centre and the selected Fit mode; do not use a maximum-count prefilter;
5. after a lawful tier is considered, search the next possible size/population by adding at least one magnet;
6. a balanced/symmetrical pair is desirable, but it is not a hidden filter—irregular shapes may lawfully add one magnet;
7. retain every distinct lawful count and layout, including lower-count layouts when a higher-count layout also exists;
8. keep bands independent; no cross-band count ownership or first-occurrence suppression;
9. do not generate every subset, every translated rectangle, or a million-item preliminary population unless an exposed input explicitly defines that search.

The active inputs bound the computation. Compute operates inside the current band, current count/tier and current procedure; it does not invent an exhaustive arrangement universe outside them.

Source: `Q23:2298-2338`, `Q23:3177-3220`; `M24:51-58`, `M24:123-140`, `M24:907-959`.

## 7. Neutral output and scoring

The engine is neutral:

- return all lawful results with their measured Centre, Fit and Magnet Addition evidence;
- no hidden winner;
- no max-count prefilter;
- no score-based deletion;
- no first-fit deletion;
- no count-only identity collapse;
- no cross-band suppression;
- no policy, priority or bias that is not selected in the admin panel.

**Flat/equal mode** applies the three laws without scoring and keeps all lawful results visible.
**Scoring mode** uses the admin-selected priority order to mark/order the same lawful result set; it may adapt the selected registration through that ordering, but it does not erase the alternatives.

## 9. Module ownership

- **Spec:** all configuration, options, bounds and defaults.
- **Compute:** geometry and neutral measurements needed by the current candidate and procedure. Compute may enforce universal physical eligibility such as safe-area containment; it does not rank or choose a winner.
- **Logic:** applies the active Centre/Fit/Magnet Addition or scoring rules to measurements. It contains no geometry kernel or duplicated Spec.
- **Engine:** streams the configured search and orchestrates Compute → Logic. It does not prebuild an unbounded neutral population.
- **Worker/bridge:** transport, caching and request/result wiring only.
- **UI:** renders Spec controls and all returned results; no solver logic.

Keep the existing scaffold and move/reuse proven bodies rather than creating another solver.
