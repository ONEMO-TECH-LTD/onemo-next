### Placement laws (the current rule set, assembled 08-24 19:47 → 08-25 10:16)

- **L1 · Gravity first — top flap is priority always.** *"gravity rule still stands top flap is
  priority always"* (08-25 10:08). The upper mass/segment is pinned before anything below.
- **L2 · Perimeter pinning, 96mm law.** *"unheld are between extreme perimeter is fine edges
  unprotected is bad every minimum 96mm of perimeter must be pinned"* (08-25 10:03). Unheld AREA
  between extremes is acceptable; unpinned EDGE runs are the failure. Max unpinned perimeter run
  ≤ 96mm is the criterion — replaces area-based ranking.
- **L3 · Segment-edge priority.** *"prioritise segment edges and not leave any segment or edge
  unprotected … we have bottom right corner exposed we can move center magnet there instead and we
  have top unprotected we need to move top row there and scale shape to fit them perio[d]"*
  (08-25 10:16). A magnet in the middle of a segment is wasted.
- **L4 · Count is exact — no silent drop in the solver.** *"it must not be silent drop it must be
  exact number set just different layout and seat filled"* (08-25 10:00). N asked = N placed; the
  frame/size/seats move to deliver it.
- **L5 · Perimeter is the manufacturing default — no interior magnets.** *"in manufacturing we are
  not gonna use internal magnets especially on bigger shapes and perimeter layout is our default …
  9 point square will have either mid magnet removed or all mid magnets removed to become 96mm
  sparse layout"* (08-24 ~23:38, mid-turn). Two ruled thinnings of a 3×3: the 8-ring, or all mids
  removed → four corners = the 96mm sparse layout.
  - Clarified 08-25 09:57: the *toggle's* subtractive count-drop (ask 9 get 8) **is correct
    behaviour** — *"it is correct behavior we dont need filling center magnets we need to pin
    perimeter along the edges - simple."* (Reconciles with L4: the perimeter belt subtracts; the
    solver's count contract is met by moving frame/size, not by populating interiors.)
- **L6 · 1–2 magnet exception.** *"only when we have 1 and 2 magnets - the centering or diagonal
  positioning of pairs to protect balance and edges must be implemented"* (08-25 10:16).
- **L7 · Centring order (Dan's four rules, 08-24 19:47).** *"1. Put the group centre on the shape
  centroid. 2. Shrink/wrap from that centred position. 3. Shift only by the minimum amount
  necessary to obtain a lawful tighter wrap. 4. Prefer the tight solution closest to the centroid —
  not the smallest shape at any centering cost."* Also 19:26: *"centroid guides selection; it never
  forces enlargement or overrides contact"* — centre is a qualifier, not a rigid gate.
- **L8 · Duck exemplar (the acceptance picture).** 3 magnets: third goes to the head at ~143mm, not
  the neck at 125 (08-25 09:59). 4 magnets: pair per mass at ~147mm. 6 magnets: three stacked pairs
  at ~192mm, waist row skipped — *"the next size with 6 magnets will look like this, not like
  this [5-in-body 245mm]"* (08-25 10:10). Scale the shape slightly to fit the top seat rather than
  cram the middle.
- **L9 · More magnets ≠ bigger shape.** *"more magnets is not necesserily bigger shape … instead of
  adding 1 more magnet and growing the shape to next possible rung to fit perfectly and cover the
  flap the engine jumps 100mm"* (08-24 22:18). Next rung, not a leap; don't miss the 2–4 good
  intermediate options.
- **L10 · Flap identifier is the enforcer.** *"teach engine to read the fucking flap areas"*
  (08-24 22:31); priority tasks 22:21: *"1. use flap detector you setup to force layout … 2. the
  perimeter toggle must be returning proper smart returns with extremes as priority pinned
  3. centering must also work to fucking fix lopsided layouts and again the flap identifier may be
  enforcer here."* Flap moves from read-out to driver of layout.
- **L11 · The named unsolved problem (Dan, 08-24 22:31):** *"we create sizes on the basis of the
  magnet count first and this then shows where the areas can become flap - we do not determine in
  advance how to layout to not create flap areas."* Layout must be determinable in advance.
- **L12 · Registration/slide bound.** Off-centre 109mm ruled absurd by screenshots; earlier: shift
  allowed *"just not too far"* (08-24 19:36). Agreed bound (from canon, kept as reuse): one 48×48
  period. Decided-not-built.

### Classification (agreed 08-24 23:17–23:28)

- **Class = the axis pair `cx × cy`**, band = dominant axis. **Slim = minor axis 1–2, standard =
  3–4** (Dan verbatim 23:20: *"3-4 is standard and 1-2 slim"*), square = cx=cy.
- **Three primitive families** (Dan 23:26): *"rectangle and square are just basic shapes made of
  square - circles and ovals are rounded versions of them - triangle and diamond are similar and T
  and L shapes are all triangle group."* → SQUARE (full field) · ROUND (square counts + corner
  padding) · TRIANGLE (partial frame: apex/corners/chains/diagonals). Diamond = double triangle,
  takes diagonal layouts (08-24 23:07 mid-turn).
- **Band by frame, not by count.** Dan floated count-bands (22:43 *"instead of banding by size we
  can band by number of magnets?"*, 22:47 *"1-4/5-9/10-16/17-25"*, 22:48 *"or 1-4/4-9/9-16/16-25"*)
  → resolved 08-25 10:23–10:26: count can't set size (*"2 magnets can produce 96mm size apart so we
  are missing smaller sizes"*); the frame carries a count range and has one size floor; classified
  shape → expected layout × count × size range, recounted with the perimeter rule.
- Open per Dan: whether band boundaries change (*"maybe we need to change band to 2 bands instead
  or i dont know"* — undecided).
