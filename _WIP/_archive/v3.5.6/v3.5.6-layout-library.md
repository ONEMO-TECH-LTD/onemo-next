> Nothing here is law until Dan prunes and blesses it. The generator held only five patterns

## Universal primitives

The **single** and the **pair** (vertical · horizontal · both diagonals) are tried in every
band regardless of class — Dan's 1–2 magnet exception: centring and diagonal placement judge
them, not the perimeter law.
