## Blocker — a certified but non-offered rung verifies as manufacture-authoritative

The creation path correctly reconstructs full per-rung offer authority in `manufacturing-spec.ts:9-16`. The verification path does not. `verifier.ts:63-70` invokes only the resolved artifact's `certifySizeSolution` for `spec.targetDominantMm`, then rebuilds the payload from that individually accepted size. `ResolvedLogicArtifact` exposes no full solve or equivalent band-offer operation (`verifier.ts:11-20`).

Independent public-caller probe:

- registered one B1 profile with two rungs, 48 mm and 60 mm;
- both rungs independently returned `ACCEPTED`;
- `SMALLEST_ACCEPTED_PER_BAND` correctly offered 48 mm;
- constructed a canonical Engine ManufacturingSpec from the accepted 60 mm solution, original source identity and pinned artifacts;
- recomputed its public canonical hash;
- both `verifyEngineManufacturingSpec` and the actual Next `verifyOnServer` caller returned `valid:true`, with minimum margin 18 mm.

This violates the Group 5 requirement for **full decision recomputation** (`03-engine-audit-and-fix-list.md:175-180`) and the creation contract that a ManufacturingSpec represents a user-selected offered solution (`02-logic-engine-spec.md:887-894`). A self-consistent non-offer must not become manufacturing authority.

Required correction:

1. Make the resolved Logic artifact expose the authoritative full solve/band-offer decision, or an equivalent complete per-rung proof operation.
2. During verification, reconstruct the band offer from the stored canonical source under the pinned profile, Compute and Logic artifacts.
3. Require the reconstructed offered solution to equal the entire stored solution/payload before returning valid.
4. Add the exact two-rung regression: 48 and 60 accepted, 48 offered; a canonical 60 mm spec must fail with a stable offer/decision mismatch code. The 48 mm spec remains the positive control.

Do not add offer history to React, introduce another verifier, or pull Group 6/7 work forward.
