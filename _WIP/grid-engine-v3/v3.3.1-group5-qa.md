This violates the Group 5 requirement for **full decision recomputation** (`03-engine-audit-and-fix-list.md:175-180`) and the creation contract that a ManufacturingSpec represents a user-selected offered solution (`02-logic-engine-spec.md:887-894`). 3. Require the reconstructed offered solution to equal the entire stored solution/payload before returning valid.
4. Add the exact two-rung regression: 48 and 60 accepted, 48 offered; a canonical 60 mm spec must fail with a stable offer/decision mismatch code. The 48 mm spec remains the positive control.
