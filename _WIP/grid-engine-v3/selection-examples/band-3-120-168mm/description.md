# Band 3 — 120–168mm — RULED BY EXAMPLE (in progress, Dan 2026-08-13)

## DUCK · 152mm · 4 magnets · MID ROW SKIPPED

`expected-duck-152mm-4mag-skip-mid-row.png` — Dan verbatim: "skipping mid row".

**The expected result:** four magnets — a pair in the head, a pair in the body — with the MIDDLE
lattice row skipped, because its sites cross the neck/waist. The four held discs form the corners
of a 48 × 96mm rectangle on the SAME lattice: skipping a row is lawful and introduces no new grid
(same principle as the diagonal pair, Dan 12:59).

**Why this placement:** head pair satisfies gravity (top held), body pair anchors the mass, the
skipped row is where the material narrows. Escalated here from band 2, where three magnets left
head flap and four did not fit (see band-2 rejected example).

## BAT-WOMAN · 144mm · 3 magnets · UTMOST CORNERS ONLY

`expected-batwoman-144mm-3mag-utmost-corners.png` — Dan verbatim: "same story with batman shape it
is more triangular and needs essentially 3 magnets utmost corners only mid 2 rows are optional".

**The expected result:** a TRIANGULAR shape takes a triangular hold — three magnets at the shape's
utmost corners: one at the top (head — gravity), two at the base corners (the wide bottom). The
middle rows are OPTIONAL: their sites may hold but are not required for the answer. The essential
set is the extreme-corner triangle.

Further band-3 shapes land as Dan walks them — do not invent the unwalked rows.
