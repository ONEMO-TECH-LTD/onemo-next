## Blocker 1 — possible-cell bridges can receive certified topology

`components.ts` sets `topologyCertified` when the possibly occupied component contains exactly one connected group of definite cells. That does not prove that the exact safe set has one component: possible-only cells may join a definite component to another exact region across a sub-diameter connector.

Independent counterexample: a broad left box, a 24 mm right box and a 23.8 mm-wide connecting neck, queried at radius 12 mm with a 6 mm sample step. The right-box centre `(32,0)` is exactly legal, while the neck has no legal centre path. The exact safe set therefore has two components. The implementation returns one merged sampled component with:

```text
topologyCertified: true
hierarchy exactness: CERTIFIED_APPROXIMATE
Logic structural status: CERTIFIED
exact witnesses: left component only
```

This silently collapses the isolated exact safe point into the left region and contradicts the structural contract.

Required repair: certify topology only when the definite/possible evidence proves the same component partition. Any possible-only bridge, island or split that can change that partition must remain indeterminate. Add this dumbbell/near-diameter-neck fixture through Compute and Logic.

## Blocker 2 — clearance-level masks wrap after 32 levels

`levelMask` and `definiteLevelMask` use JavaScript bitwise shifts. `1 << 32` aliases `1 << 0`. The public profile schema accepts more than 32 strictly increasing clearance levels, and the R3 Compute contract supplies no 32-level product cap.

Independent registered-profile probe: a 40 mm square has maximum clearance 20 mm. With 33 approved clearance levels and a 5 mm sample step, the level at radius 44 mm incorrectly returns a component with:

```text
topologyCertified: true
area bounds: [100,400] mm²
maximum-clearance upper bound: 21.0355 mm
Logic structural status: CERTIFIED
```

Required repair: replace the 32-bit masks with a per-level representation that cannot alias. Do not invent an unapproved 31-level cap. Add a regression with at least 33 levels proving that no occupied component is reported above the polygon's certified maximum clearance.
