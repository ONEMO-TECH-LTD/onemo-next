## Complexity verdict

- Contained: safety rounding, profile bypass, schema invariants, canonical ordering, top-direction use, physical validation.
- Medium: parity/permission model, source identity propagation, overlay and adapter compilation, build locking.
- High: conservative structural hierarchy, compound/anchored continuous optimisation, complete representable tie-break, semantic ManufacturingSpec reverification.
- Highest execution risk: certified all-band completeness under the 16 ms gate.

The current architecture remains valid. The work is difficult because several modules currently expose the right names while implementing weaker semantics; replacement is required inside those modules, not around them.

## Necessity and sufficiency

**Necessity:** every item above maps directly to a reproduced audit failure or mandatory R3 gate. No fourth package, new engine, donor extraction, parallel selector, or additional planning layer is needed.

**Sufficiency:** completing all eight groups, then passing independent QA and live verification, delivers the engineering contract. Unavailable product-owned calibration, Batwoman geometry and physical process values remain explicit non-production inputs; they do not justify weakening or deferring the engineering repairs.
