Suede in 2D · four recipes, one artwork

Each card adds one thing to the one before. A is your Suede Studio spec (2 Nov). Move the mouse over D, or tap "tilt" on a phone, to move its light. Same card size as the shop feed (304).

A · your spec
Artwork + your nap and wash grain (fractal lit, density 13, size 1.55, detail 8, seed 32, light 46°/63°, relief 4.2, wash 30) at 100% overlay + 12% sheen. What the Figma card uses now.
B · A + printed on suede
The art is treated as dye in fabric: colour softened, blacks lifted (suede can't print true black), a hair of blur. Grain unchanged.
C · B + real suede fibres
Adds the real suede scan's fibre map (GSG SuedeBlack, the 3D viewer's material), lit once from your light angle. Your nap and wash stay on top.
D · C, relit live
Same as C, but the light follows the mouse or the phone's tilt, so the fibres catch and lose light like real suede.
