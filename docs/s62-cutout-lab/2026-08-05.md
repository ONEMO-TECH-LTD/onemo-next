## F13 · 15:00 — what the AI must NOT be asked to do (architecture, still binding)
> Q: can the AI do the simplification/fine-tuning as part of the prompt?

**No — and it shouldn't.** SAM does **segmentation only**. Simplify/offset/smooth are **geometric
post-processing**: instant (<5 ms), exactly tunable, free, reproducible. A better *model* buys a
cleaner starting mask — it never replaces the geometry step.

**And the loop shape Dan proposed, confirmed correct:**
- **Live loop (re-runs on change):** detect (AI) → clean → simplify/offset — instant, no AI for the knobs.
- **Compositing + blend = LAST, separate step — NOT re-run on each change.**
→ *This is the direct ancestor of the v1 Cadence Law and of v2's `ComposeScheduler`
(`src/lib/bridge-compose-policy/index.ts`) — compose never runs mid-drag, only on release/idle.*

## D15 · 15:52–16:15 — mobile reality kicks in
> *"It doesn't work on mobile it doesn't load the page"* → *"upload is stuck - also on mobile we need
> responsive at minimum stacking layout"* → *"no the image is not uploading in safari"* → *"in mac as well"*

**Two distinct defects, correctly separated:**
1. **"Stuck" = main-thread inference.** The probe ran the AI on the main thread, so a ~6–30 s WASM
   encode **froze the tab**. Fix: move inference to a **Web Worker** — which is the production shape
   anyway. → *this is why every later lab build keeps the model in a worker.*
2. **Image wouldn't upload in Safari at all (Mac too), worked in Chrome** — NOT the freeze.
   Cause: `RawImage.read()` ran **before** the image was drawn, plus `createImageBitmap` /

   immediately, hand **raw pixels** to the worker; no `RawImage.read`/`createImageBitmap` on either
   side. Plus responsive stacking for phones.

> ⚠️ **v2 note:** on 2026-08-07 the *opposite* failure appeared — `img.decode()` **hangs
> unresolved** on large blob images and the fix was a load-event await
> (`src/lib/engine-matte-input/index.ts`). Both facts are true: `decode()` is the Safari-safe way to
> get pixels, but it must not be awaited unguarded on big blobs. **Anyone touching image loading
> must respect both.**
