### F15 · 16:00–16:21 — **what can and cannot be boosted** (the ceiling probe, concluded)
| knob | ceiling | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Smooth** | handle factor maxes at 1 (beyond it curves overshoot and the fold-guard rejects) | v1 claimed **boostable by REPETITION** (`dac00ee1`, a second pass, later 200) — **but see the correction below: repetition is a NO-OP** |
| **Radius** | 100 % = half the shape's short side = a square becomes a circle | geometric max, nothing beyond |
| **Offset** | physical mm; the cap is a product choice | Dan's 15 % |
| **Detail** | at 0 you are already at the raw trace (ceiling = the model's map resolution) | **only coarser has headroom** |
| **Straighten / Simplify** | tolerance can rise but **saturates** | small headroom, quickly exhausted |

**The honest finding at 16:15:** *smooth 50 and smooth 150 render pixel-identical on a raw dense
trace* — **Smooth rounds *between anchors*, and a full-detail AI trace has hundreds of micro-segments,
so the rounding energy disperses and vanishes.** Dan's counter-evidence — *"smooth worked on 150
better so 200 should be fine not dead"* — reconciles it exactly: **Smooth bites in proportion to how
sparse the anchors are**, and his workflow reduces (Detail/Simplify) first. The "dead" claim was true
only for the untouched raw trace. `8b449cc4` removed the multipliers; the probe had concluded.

> **⚠️ CORRECTION (2026-08-08, measured on the v2 tree during KAI-10206).** v1's "Smooth boostable by
> repetition" is **wrong** — a second/third pass is a **no-op by construction**, and v1's own 16:15
> probe already showed it (`smooth 50 == 150`), yet v1 shipped the multi-pass anyway. Proof: `smoothPaper`
> computes catmull handles **from anchor POSITIONS**, and the kernel's own comment says catmull *"only
> sets handles (never adds/moves points)"* — so re-running it re-derives the identical handles from the
> identical positions. Measured on v2: smooth **50 / 100 / 150 / 200 render the identical area**. The
> real lever is anchor **sparsity** (reduce first with Detail/Simplify), not pass count — which is the
> half of the 16:15 finding that was right. **Do not re-apply the multi-pass in v2** (it was on the
> KAI-10206 plan and withdrawn on this measurement).

### F16 · 16:35 — **why Detail "overrides" everything applied after it** ⚖ STANDING
> **Detail is not an adjustment — it is a generation-level control.** Any change **re-derives the
> outline from the raw AI trace**, and only then do simplify → smooth → radius re-apply on that fresh
> basis. Touching Detail after tuning does not stack; it rebuilds the foundation underneath.
> Additionally, at coarse Detail the rebuilt outline is a clean sparse polygon, and **Simplify's own
> guard ("a clean polygon is left alone" — the invariant that keeps squares square) makes it a
> silent no-op.**
> **Practical rule: set Detail FIRST, then shape with Simplify/Smooth/Radius.**

### F19 · 17:12 — **the insect-bite fix: use the engine's own mask smoothing** (`01ef60f8`)

### F20 · 17:00 — **repeated strokes degraded because the soft matte went stale** (`679e15cb`)
