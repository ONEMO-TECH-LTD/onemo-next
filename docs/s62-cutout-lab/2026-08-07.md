> Dan: *"No still internal imperfections on the first Sam Generation"*

> ⚖ **A binary mask and a soft matte are two different fields. A hole guard on one does not fix the other.**

## Part 4 — 14:56–15:22: the research that decided the final stack

### F11 · **u2net is genuinely NOT promptable — proven from our own ONNX bytes**
> Dan: *"can we verify if u2 net is not really promptable maybe we are missing this part in u2 net
> research"* → then *"maybe u2 has feature to add shapes on reruns … also check siluetta … lets
> explore from the original source code"*

- **Our own models, introspected:** `u2netp.onnx` and `silueta.onnx` each expose **a single image
  input** and one saliency output (`ben.worker.ts:159–161`). `edgesam.decoder` is the only one carrying
  `point_coords`/`point_labels`. **Silueta is a quantized u2net — same architecture.**
- **Literature check** (because Dan's instinct deserved it, not a dismissal): U²-Net (Qin et al. 2020)
  is a feed-forward salient-object-detection net. The 2025 papers that pair "prompt" with SOD use
  **learned prompt tokens baked in at training** to fuse RGB/depth/thermal — **not user clicks**.
- **rembg's real precision tools exist and we don't use them:** **alpha matting** (build a trimap by
  eroding the mask, closed-form matting on the unknown band) and `post_process_mask`. **Honest
  correction recorded:** alpha matting only softens a thin band around the *existing* boundary — **it
  cannot pull in a missed region.**

### D5 · 15:13 — **GrabCut is the answer, and it was already in the bundle** ⚖ STANDING
> Dan: *"any background removal tool nowadays has this precision 'paint over the objects you want to
> be on the foreground roughly and model recognises the edges'"*

**The UX has a name: interactive/scribble-guided segmentation.** Two families:
1. **Deep** (Photoshop Select&Mask, SAM/SAM2, RITM/SimpleClick/FocalClick) — heavy.
2. **Classic deterministic — GrabCut** (Microsoft Research, 2004): *mark the foreground roughly,
   iterated graph-cuts snap to the true colour/contrast edges.* No deep model, CPU-only, milliseconds
   on a small region — **and `grabCut` + `GC_FGD` were already in the OpenCV.js the wand loaded.**

**The honest ranking, recorded:** **wand (dumb, instant) < GrabCut (edge-aware, light, guided) <
u2net/SAM (semantic, heavy).** GrabCut gives ~80–90 % of the "rough paint → clean edge" experience;
deep models win only on thin wires and fur against similar colour.

### F12 · 15:18 — **the reflective floor: why the robot's between-legs filled green** ⚠️
Reproduced deterministically: **with a reflective floor connecting the feet, the between-legs
background becomes an *enclosed* pocket**, and the uncapped hole-fill floods it.
> ⚠️ **A dropout HOLE and a between-legs VOID are both "enclosed background." A border-flood fill
> cannot tell them apart.** That is the flaw in the "uncapped fill is safe" call Meta and I both made.

> **The diagnosis is right anyway, and this is why it matters:** the fallback only fires when the

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE DELETION LAW — the single most important lesson of the day:**
> **A feature is not deleted until every READER of its interface is gone AND every artifact it served
> is removed.** Deleting the writer while the reader lives leaves a live path that stale clients still
> take. **Deletion-completeness must be tested from a STALE/STAMPED client, not a clean URL.**
> *(Meta named its own QA fault on the record: it gated deslop-completeness on a lab-only grep and a
