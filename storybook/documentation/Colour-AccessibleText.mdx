<H2>On imagery</H2>

<P>
  A photograph does not theme-flip, so its text must not either. The candidates are the pinned pure
  extremes; sampled region luminance decides which one speaks. Essential copy additionally sits on the
  image scrim, so legibility never depends on the photograph cooperating.
</P>

<ImagePickDemo />

<H2>On colour</H2>

<P>
  A colour surface prefers its own family's voice: the deep reading on light steps, the pale reading on deep
  steps — colour-native pairings rather than raw black or white. Neutral candidates enter only as fallback
  for the few mid-tone surfaces where neither of the family's own voices reaches body-text contrast; those
  surfaces carry short, large or bold type only.
</P>

<H2>Where it runs</H2>

<P>
  The algorithm ships as a small pure function used by both this documentation and the product engine, so
  the law documented here and the law executed in the app are the same code path.
</P>

<Note>
  Candidate order encodes design intent, not just mathematics: family voices before neutrals means the
  system prefers colour-matched text wherever it is legal, and falls back loudly rather than silently.
</Note>
