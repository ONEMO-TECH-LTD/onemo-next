<H2>app/ — the canvases and their world</H2>

<P>
  App surfaces are peer canvases picked per context, not a default with variants — so this one family
  carries ordinal names: <C>primary</C> is the ground the editor stands on (the surface pair, silver in
  the light world), with <C>secondary</C>, <C>tertiary</C> and <C>quaternary</C> stepping through the
  grey zones. <C>veil</C>, the blur-free wash, sits beside them. Theme and constancy suffixes compose on
  the ordinals as everywhere (<C>primary-contrast</C> opposes the theme on the same pair,{' '}
  <C>primary-perma</C> pins the light-world reading).
</P>

<BgFolderTable folder="app" />

<H2>control/ — the interactive zone</H2>

<P>
  The fills of things you can touch. The rest fill rides the voice pair's paper side; <C>subtle</C>,{' '}
  <C>strong</C> and <C>solid</C> step the supporting fills, and the pinned contrast ladder —{' '}
  <C>contrast-perma-primary</C> through <C>-quaternary</C> plus <C>-absolute</C> — carries the permanently
  dark furniture, anchored so it never flips. Transient states are washes over the rest fill, never colour
  steps; persistent engagement is a component contract.
</P>

<BgFolderTable folder="control" />

<H2>frost/ and the materials</H2>

<P>
  Translucent materials carry only their tint here — the Snow alpha ladder, adaptive by construction
  (paper wash in light, ink wash in dark). Blur strength and blend construction are authored natively on
  the node and translated structurally by the converter; no filter tokens exist. <C>veil</C> is the same
  wash without glass.
</P>

<H2>max/ — the extremes</H2>

<P>
  The full peer-family set, same shape as the border tier: pure and brand extremes, each composing with
  theme and constancy. Surfaces read light-first — <C>default</C> is the light world's own extreme and{' '}
  <C>default-contrast</C> the opposing one — and the pure float role lives on <C>default</C>: roles may
  share values, so one token carries the lifted white surface and the pure extreme alike. max is the
  semantic reserve; contexts with their own names (app, control) own their specific uses.
</P>

<BgFolderTable folder="max" />

<H2>brand/ and system/</H2>

<P>
  Coloured surfaces per colour — bare tint, <C>-strong</C>, <C>-solid</C> — with the neutral-first law:
  tints are the default coloured surface. The platform's surfaces mirror the same trio per status, plus
  the one grey disabled fill.
</P>

<H2>What deliberately is not here</H2>

<P>
  <b>Card and sheet are components, not surface classes</b> — their look is a component contract binding
  these semantic surfaces, minted at the component tier when those components exist. <b>Hover carries no
  tokens</b> — interaction is the wash system's job. Missing combinations mint lazily at their
  predictable names; the grammar guarantees the name before the token exists.
</P>
