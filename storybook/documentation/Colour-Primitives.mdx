<H2>Adaptive families</H2>

<P>
  Each family is a twelve-step scale following the Radix composition: the first steps are backgrounds, the
  middle steps are component fills and borders, the deep steps are solids and text. The two strips per row
  are the same family's light and dark faces — the flip the whole system inherits.
</P>

<AdaptiveFamilies />

<H2>Base tones</H2>

<P>
  The pure extremes and the brand ink/paper pair. The pair flips into each other across themes — the brand's
  own black and white, distinct from the grey scale's endpoints.
</P>

<BaseTones />

<H2>Behavior groups</H2>

<P>
  Beyond the adaptive families, each pigment exists in behavior variants. The group prefix says how a value
  responds to the theme; the family and step inside it stay the same. Six groups cover every combination of
  transparency and anchoring the product needs.
</P>

<H2>Transparency — <C>alpha/</C></H2>

<P>
  Every solid step has a transparent twin: the most transparent colour that still composites to exactly the
  solid over its background. Twins let one token read correctly over photos, glass and gradients while
  matching the palette on flat surfaces. The proof renders live below — each pill is half solid, half twin
  over the strip's background; a visible seam would mean a mismatch.
</P>

<TwinProof family="grey" />

<P>
  At fully-saturated steps the mathematics collapses honestly: no transparent colour over white can produce
  a colour with an empty channel, so the twin degenerates to the solid. This is canon, not a defect — the
  twin's promise is the exact match, and transparency is delivered wherever the colour allows it.
</P>

<H2>Anchored constants — <C>l-constant/</C> · <C>d-constant/</C></H2>

<P>
  Constants keep a colour's identity when the theme flips: the step keeps meaning the same position of the
  scale instead of following the theme's role-flip. Two anchors exist so either end of the scale can be the
  origin of counting.
</P>

<AnchorDemo family="grey" />

<H2>Anchored transparency — <C>l-alpha/</C> · <C>d-alpha/</C></H2>

<P>
  The transparent counterpart of the anchored constants: washes that keep being the same colour when the
  theme flips. The base-tone ladders inside this group run a fixed opacity sequence — the workhorses for
  scrims and photo overlays, where the imagery underneath never theme-flips.
</P>

<AlphaLadder path="l-alpha/base/black" />

<H2>Mode-swapped — <C>inverse/</C></H2>

<P>
  The adaptive ramp with its faces exchanged: the light face carries the dark value and vice versa. This is
  how coloured tokens serve surfaces that invert against the theme — the mono pair gets inversion free by
  swapping members; colours get it from this group.
</P>

<InverseDemo family="blue-green" />

<Note>
  Group prefixes are the primitive tier's grammar: <C>behavior/family/step</C>. The alias tier re-expresses
  the same behaviors colour-first — see <b>Colour → Aliases</b>.
</Note>
