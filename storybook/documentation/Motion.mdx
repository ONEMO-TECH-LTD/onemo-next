<H2>The cascade</H2>

<P>
  Motion follows the same three tiers as colour: raw time and easing primitives (with scale and spring
  factors in the ratio primitives), an alias vocabulary, and the semantic set below. Values exist exactly
  once; retuning the product's feel is one edit at the primitive tier.
</P>

<H2>The sources</H2>

<P>
  The spring pair is the platform default spring of the native canon; the snappy pair is its small-control
  class; the duration ladder and curves align with the Material motion canon where the two agree. The press
  scale is tuned a hair subtler than the native pattern for ONEMO's flatter language. Springs ship as raw
  response and damping parameters — JS animation consumes them directly, and baked CSS spring curves
  generate at converter time.
</P>

<H2>What stays native-only</H2>

<P>
  Haptic patterns, the platform keyboard curve, and refractive glass effects have no honest web delivery —
  they are documented as native behaviors, never tokenized. Scroll feel approximates through standard
  overscroll behavior.
</P>

<Note>
  States pair with motion at the component tier: a press is the press wash plus the press scale in one
  contract. The wash carries the tint; this collection carries the physics.
</Note>
