<H2>The contrast-role law</H2>

<P>
  Material names (Ink, Snow) and lightness names (dark, light) anchor to the light world and go false when
  the theme flips. <C>max</C> is the one word that stays true in both themes — it names contrast against
  the current surface, not a colour. Where the mono pair appears as peers — lines, surfaces — it lives in
  the <C>max</C> folder: bare <C>max</C> is the pure extreme, <C>max/brand</C> the brand extreme, with{' '}
  <C>-inverse</C> and <C>-perma</C> composing as everywhere. Families with a dominant default (text,
  glyphs) keep role names instead — prominence is the point of a picker.
</P>

<P>
  The grey ladder underneath follows the ramp convention: separators low, interactive boundaries mid,
  focus-adjacent lines high — with the register words (<C>-subtle</C>, <C>-strong</C>, <C>-muted</C>)
  selecting steps, never the constancy words.
</P>
