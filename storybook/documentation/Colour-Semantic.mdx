<H2>The naming grammar</H2>

<P>
  <b>Family</b> says whose voice speaks: the pure extremes, the brand ink/paper voice, or a brand colour.{' '}
  <b>Register</b> modifies loudness — <C>-subtle</C> quiets a family the way <C>-strong</C> amplifies it.{' '}
  <b>Behavior</b> says how the role responds to the theme: a bare name follows it, <C>-inverse</C> opposes
  it, <C>-dark</C> and <C>-light</C> pin one of the family's voices so it never flips. Because the words
  describe voices rather than pigments, greys and colours adopt the same suffixes without new vocabulary.
</P>

<P>
  Every sample below renders on the role's real surface — the flipping button, the constant-ink pill, the
  imagery stand-in — with the left panel in the light theme and the right in the dark.
</P>
