<H2>DS v2.3.2 — the colour rebuild</H2>

<P><b>2026-07-12 · Aluminium the surface.</b> Third base material minted — the anodized slab the editor
stands on — as <C>base/brand-aluminium</C> with the full behavior grid at both plumbing tiers
(<C>brand/alu</C>: constants, three alpha ladders, explicit inverse — partnerless pairs store their swap
in the <C>inverse/</C> section). The editor ground re-bound onto it.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-12 · Backgrounds executed and sealed.</b> The <C>bg/</C> family rebuilt under the grammar:
app canvases on ordinal names with chrome and veil folded in, the interactive control trio with the dead
hover step, four frost grades plus veil as native-authored materials, the full eight-cell <C>max</C> peer
set, brand and system packs. Card and sheet ruled component-tier; the elevated role removed as a duplicate
of the pure extreme opposing the theme — roles may share values, one token carries the reading.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-12 · Effects tier built alias-only.</b> The universal strength range (<C>fx/</C>, groups ×
sizes onto existing dimension steps), the semantic blur ladder in one intensity register, shadow and
light tint ladders — occlusion pinned dark, emission pinned light — over grouped alias tint ranges.
Filter tokens rejected as law: blends and filters are node facts the converter translates structurally.
The collection's redundant dark mode removed.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-12 · Border roles executed.</b> The <C>border/</C> family under the contrast-role law:
peer families name the mono pair inside <C>max/</C> (<C>max</C> pure, <C>max/brand</C> the brand
extreme); the register ladder on ramp steps; the <C>/default</C> qualifier applied across families with
variant folders.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-11 · Glyph tier mirrored.</b> <C>fg/</C> rebuilt to mirror <C>text/</C>'s combination set
one-to-one on the same alias routes; the brand colour packs and the pinned/inverse variants completed on
the glyph side. The last state-word token (a hover that was really the decorative register) renamed to its
honest name with its consumer preserved — glyph hover is a wash, never a token. Remaining tier
differences each carry a physical reason (contrast floor, muted tier, solid graphics) and render as a
computed comparison on the Semantic roles page.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-11 · Vocabulary sealed: materials, perma grammar, picker folders.</b> Material names canonized
(Ink · Snow · Aluminium). Constancy became an orthogonal suffix — <C>-perma</C> pins the light-world
reading, <C>-inverse-perma</C> the dark-world reading — replacing the earlier <C>-dark</C>/<C>-light</C>
suffixes, which conflated value-choice with pinning. Semantic families regrouped into picker folders
(<C>primary/ · max/ · brand/ · system/</C>); the glyph tier (<C>fg/</C>) rebuilt and locked under the same
grammar. Durable statement: <C>token-vocabulary.md</C> in the design-system SSOT.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-11 · Semantic text roles executed.</b> The <C>text/</C> family rebuilt from 23 legacy tokens to
33 roles under the family × register × behavior grammar: mono four-packs (pure extremes + brand voice), a
supporting register on the transparency ladders, per-brand-colour behavior packs (subtle, inverse, dark,
light), and statuses re-anchored for legibility. Fifteen legacy names retired with every consumer
re-pointed before deletion; two deliberate visible fixes (an illegible placeholder tier and a
tone-on-same-tone role). Verified by full cascade read-back.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-13 · Pair naming law.</b> Base materials renamed as face-order pairs — ink-snow, snow-ink,
silver-space, space-silver, white-black, black-white — with single-colour names reserved for pinned
constants. Aluminium retired from token vocabulary; the mono pairs' duplicate pinned swaps purged in
favour of the partner's constants.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-13 · Mechanism parents and the mirror rule.</b> Primitive behaviors reorganised into nine
top-level parents (bare defaults, base pairs, alpha, inverse, inverse-alpha, constant, constant-alpha,
inverse-constant, inverse-constant-alpha), each replicating the top-level layout inside. Alias tier
flattened colour-first with dash-composed pair and face names; the plumbing vocabulary keeps mechanical
"inverse" while the semantic tier speaks contrast where opposition is mono-routed.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-13 · Semantic grammar completed.</b> Register always explicit (default- prefix on the default
register's variants); quiet registers moved onto grey steps (transparency reserved for see-through roles);
colour-routed opposition renamed inverse; the border hairline register named; bg/max flipped to light-first
polarity with consumers byte-preserved.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-13 · Motion tokens.</b> Full cascade minted — time and easing primitives, ratio factors, alias
vocabulary, and the semantic motion set (press scale, duration ladder, easing curves, spring pairs) —
code-facing on the breakpoints precedent. Picker scopes enforced per property across every collection;
stroke fields offer border tokens exclusively.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-11 · Inverse primitive group.</b> Mode-swapped copies of all adaptive ramps added as a new
behavior group, giving coloured tokens a true opposite-of-theme behavior; alias routes added across the
palette.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-11 · Anchored groups and brand pair.</b> Light- and dark-anchored constants and transparencies
(anchor-by-counting: the dark-anchored group reads the same values from the opposite end) replaced the
single constant group; the brand ink/paper pair corrected to flip into each other rather than into the
grey ramp's endpoints.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-10 · Alias tier rebuilt colour-first.</b> The palette tier restructured to
<C>colour/qualifier/step</C> with full behavior collections per palette colour; behavior-first names and
suffix conventions retired. Plumbing tiers (primitives, aliases) de-scoped so the picker exposes semantic
roles only.</P>

<P><b>2026-07-10 · Transparency system.</b> Solved-twin alpha ramps generated for every family and validated
byte-exact against the reference implementation; opacity ladders added for the base tones; saturated-step
twins accepted as opaque by mathematical necessity.</P>
